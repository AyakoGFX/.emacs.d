*Waxing Moon* (Shukla Paksha):
- The Moon is moving away from the Sun, heading towards 180°.
- Vedic term: Shukla Paksha

*Waning Moon* (Krishna Paksha):
- The Moon has passed the opposite point (Full Moon) and is heading back.
- Vedic term: Krishna Paksha
